# LearnAI

**Lifelong AI education system for every human — from age 3 to old age.**

LearnAI is transitioning from a marketplace-first tutoring product into **YourTeacher by LearnAI**: a learner-first platform where safe AI teachers adapt to age, language, goals, and progress.

## New Product Direction

### Mission

> Make personal education available to every human through safe AI teachers that adapt to the learner's age, goals, attention, and progress.

### Positioning

- **Repository name:** LearnAI
- **Product brand:** YourTeacher by LearnAI
- **Tagline:** Your personal AI teacher from first words to PhD.

## Core Platform Engines

```text
LearnAI
├── Learner Profile Engine
├── Curriculum Engine
├── Tutor Persona Engine
├── Assessment Engine
├── Safety & Guardian Engine
└── Agent-Matrix Integration Layer
```

## MVP Focus (Phase 1)

**LearnAI Family MVP** for children (3–8) and parents:

- parent account and child profile
- age-safe AI teacher selection
- daily 5-minute micro-lessons
- progress stars and parent summaries

## Current Tech Foundation (retained)
