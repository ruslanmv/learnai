# Legacy Marketplace Context

This document preserves LearnAI's previous positioning as an AI-powered tutoring marketplace.

## Previous Focus

- matching students with human professors
- scheduling and booking live sessions
- payments and revenue split logic
- top professor discovery workflows

## Why It Is Legacy

The active product direction is now lifelong AI education, with learner-first flows and age-adaptive AI teachers as the core experience.

Marketplace features are not removed from the codebase yet, but are considered **experimental/legacy** while the platform transitions.

## Potential Future Role

Marketplace capabilities may return as a secondary **Human Mentor Layer** after the learner-first AI education engine is stable.
